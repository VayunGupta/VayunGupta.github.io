RTL verification · Verilator2026

Evidence-guided RTL debugging with LLMs

Built an AI-assisted RTL repair workflow using Verilator logs, signal-trace summaries, and automated testbenches. Evaluated the effect of debugging evidence on five historical processor bugs, with repairs checked against hidden simulation tests.

Log and trace evidence improved successful repairs from 6/15 to 15/15 trials in the evaluated set.
